---
name: grilling
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan or design. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan before building, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrases.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Group questions into batches of up to four and ask each batch together, but only when the questions in it are genuinely independent — none of them changes what another asks, or whether it's still relevant, based on the others' answers. Wait for the whole batch to be answered before moving on.

Keep a question on its own whenever a later question's content, options, or relevance depends on it: ask it alone, wait for that answer, then continue — never batch a dependent question ahead of the answer it depends on.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the codebase, look it up rather than asking me. The *decisions*, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer.

Do not enact the plan until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
